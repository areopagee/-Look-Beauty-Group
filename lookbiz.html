Privacy Policy

Look Biz (Android app)

Effective date: November 2, 2026

Summary

Look Biz works fully offline. It has no account, no sign-in and no internet access, and it asks for no device permissions. Everything you type stays on your phone. We do not collect, receive, sell or share your information.

1. Who we are

Look Biz is published by Look Beauty Group LLC, 30 N Gould St, Sheridan, WY 82801, United States. In this policy, "we", "us" and "our" mean Look Beauty Group LLC.

2. Information you enter in the app

Look Biz is a tool for managing a small beauty business. Only the information you choose to type is stored, and only in the app on your device:

  • Loyalty stamp cards: customer names, stamp counts and rewards redeemed.
  • Quotes: item names, prices, quantities, costs, discount and tax percentages, quote titles and saved quotes.
  • Stock: product names, quantities and reorder levels.
  • Sales and expenses: amounts, notes and dates.
  • Settings such as the number of visits needed for a reward.

This information is saved in the app's private storage on your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else.

3. What we do not collect

  • We do not collect personal information, device identifiers, advertising IDs or location.
  • The app does not request any Android permissions, including camera, contacts, microphone, storage, location and notifications.
  • The app does not connect to the internet, so it cannot transmit your data.
  • The app contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or other third-party software that collects data.

4. Sharing of information

Because we do not receive your information, we do not sell, rent, share or disclose it to any third party.

5. Storage, backups and security

Your data is stored in the app's private area on your device, which other apps cannot normally read. Its safety also depends on your device, for example a screen lock and keeping your phone up to date.

Depending on your device and its settings, Android may include app data in a backup to your own Google account or your phone maker's backup service. That backup is managed by those services under their own privacy policies, not by us. You can control it in your phone's backup settings.

We cannot access, recover or restore your data for you.

6. Keeping and deleting your data

  • You can remove individual customers, items, products, quotes and transactions inside the app.
  • You can delete all app data by clearing the app's storage in your phone's settings, or by uninstalling the app.

Because we hold no copy of your data, deleting it on your device removes it completely, apart from any device backup described above.

7. Your customers' information

If you enter your own customers' names or other details, you are responsible for having a lawful reason to keep them and for following the privacy rules that apply to your business. We recommend entering only the information you need.

8. Children's privacy

Look Biz is a business tool and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children, and the app does not collect information from anyone.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, for example by adding a feature that connects to the internet, we will update this page and the effective date above before the change is released.
